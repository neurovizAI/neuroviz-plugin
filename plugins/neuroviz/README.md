# NeuroViz

Retouch jewelry and product photos, see your pieces worn on a model, and make product videos, all from your own photos, with each result shown in the chat. This plugin bundles the NeuroViz connector (`https://app.neuroviz.ai/mcp`, where you sign in with your NeuroViz account) and three skills: `retouch-jewelry-photo`, `virtual-try-on` and `product-video`. The skills tell the assistant when NeuroViz fits and how to choose the right tool for your photo.

Install steps for Claude, Claude Code, Codex and ChatGPT are in the [repository README](https://github.com/neurovizAI/neuroviz-plugin#install). For help, write to support@neuroviz.ai.
